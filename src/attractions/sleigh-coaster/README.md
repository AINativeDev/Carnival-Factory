# Sleigh-Ride Coaster

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A roller coaster with a candy-cane track on wooden trestles, running over a
small bump, a big snowy hill and a last bump under a starry sky. Santa's red
sleigh, with a sack and a present aboard, rides it from left to right every
five seconds, tilting with the track, slowing near the top of the hill and
speeding up on the way down.

Preview it at `#/attraction/sleigh-coaster`.
