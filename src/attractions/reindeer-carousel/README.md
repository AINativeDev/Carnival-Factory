# Reindeer Carousel

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A carousel under a red and white candy-cane canopy with a gold trim and a gold
ball on top. Six reindeer with gold antlers and red noses go round it on gold
poles, once every nine seconds, rising and falling as they turn. The ones at
the back are fainter and face the other way, so you can see them come round.

Preview it at `#/attraction/reindeer-carousel`.
