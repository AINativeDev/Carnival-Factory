# Gingerbread Fun House

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A gingerbread house with a chocolate roof thick with icing and a candy-cane
door. It leans and sways a little, as a fun house should. Gumdrop lights along
the roof blink on and off as a dark gap chases along the string, and its two
round mirror windows stretch and squash.

Preview it at `#/attraction/gingerbread-fun-house`.
