# Ferris Wheel

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A ferris wheel on a wooden A-frame against the night sky, hung with eight
bauble gondolas in red, gold, mint and violet. The wheel turns slowly, once
every 24 seconds, and each bauble hangs straight down from the rim as it goes
round. Two ribbons of northern lights, green and violet, ripple across the sky
behind it.

Preview it at `#/attraction/ferris-wheel`.
