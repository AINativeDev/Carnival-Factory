# Santa's Big Top

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

Santa's workshop under a big top in red and white stripes, with a gold
scalloped hem and a green pennant fluttering on the peak. Elves in pointed
green hats pop out of the tent's three flaps in turn and duck back in.

Preview it at `#/attraction/big-top`.
