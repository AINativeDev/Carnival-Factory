# Sleigh-Ride Coaster

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A game you play, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a long roller
coaster seen side on at dusk, its candy-cane near rail and shaded far rail on
wooden trestles, running over a snowy hill whose slopes are lit where they
face the light. Santa's red sleigh, with two elves riding it, a sack of
presents behind them and a warm lantern glow, rides it from the station, and
the track scrolls by as it goes.

## Boost & Brake: how to play

Step inside and tap to ride. Then **hold** to push the sleigh on, and **let
go** to coast.

- The sleigh needs speed to get over each hill: without it, it rolls back into
  the valley and you lose time.
- Too much speed over a crest flies it off the rails. It tumbles head over
  heels into a snowdrift, and the run ends there.
- In between, it lifts off the rail over the crest for a moment of airtime.
  Presents float above some of the crests, and a sleigh that flies past one at
  the right height catches it, with a "+50".
- The speed gauge in the corner reads how fast the sleigh will cross the next
  crest if you let go now, in four bands: **too slow** (blue), **roll over**
  (mint), **airtime** (gold, where the presents are) and **too fast** (red).
  The bands change with every crest, since a sharper crest throws you sooner.
- The track gets taller, steeper and bumpier the further you go, and the room
  between airtime and too fast gets narrower. The little bumps between hills
  give a hop, but never throw you off.

A run lasts 45 seconds. It scores a point for each metre from the station and
50 for each present. Along the top you see the distance, the presents and the
clock. When the clock runs out ("Time's up!") or the sleigh flies off ("Off
the rails!"), a card shows the run's distance, presents and score, and the
best score of your visit. Tap again to ride again.

With nobody playing, an elf drives: it pushes until the gauge reads airtime
and lets go, so it flies over every crest and catches the presents, and
starts again from the station every half minute. The board over the track
says "Tap to ride!".
