# Sleigh-Ride Coaster

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
roller coaster with a candy-cane rail on wooden trestles, running across a
slab of snow from its left corner to its right, over a small bump, a big snowy
hill and a last bump. The hill's slopes are lit where they face the light.
Santa's red sleigh, with two elves riding it, a sack of presents behind them
and a warm lantern glow, waits at the station by the left corner, beside a
launch lever that glows gently.

## How to play

Step inside and tap the coaster, or click it with a mouse. The sleigh leaves
the station, climbs the hill, slowing near the top, and whooshes down the
other side, tilting with the track. On each drop the elves throw their hands
up. Once it has run the whole track, it's back at the station for the next
tap: a tap while it's still riding does nothing.

Every launch lights one more bulb on the cheer meter, the post in front of the
hill. Five launches fill it: its star lights and it glows warmly, and the next
launch starts it again from one.

Preview it at `#/attraction/sleigh-coaster`.
