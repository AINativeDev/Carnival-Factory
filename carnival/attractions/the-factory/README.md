# The Factory

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

It's the landmark for The Factory, the North Pole's own level
([`carnival/levels/the-factory/`](../../levels/the-factory/)). On the home map,
a click on it, or E or Enter beside it, takes the sleigh into the level rather
than into a room. Its own page, `#/attraction/the-factory`, still shows this
drawing in its room, with a link to take the sleigh inside.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
warm, gingerbread-brown workshop with a saw-tooth roof of snowy ridges, a
chimney whose three puffs of smoke rise and fade in turn, a lit door with a
gold **Tessl** sign over it, and a belt that carries parcels out of the door.
