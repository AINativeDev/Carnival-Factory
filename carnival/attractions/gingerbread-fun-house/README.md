# Gingerbread Fun House

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
crooked gingerbread house on a slab of snow, lit on its left and shaded on its
right, with a chocolate roof edged in icing and a candy-cane door glowing warm.
It leans and sways a little, as a fun house should, and gumdrop lights chase
slowly along its eaves.

## How to play

Step inside and decorate the house. Each tap or click sticks a treat where you
point: a gumdrop, then an icing swirl, then a wrapped candy, in turn, each
popping on in one of the gumdrop colours. The treats sway with the house. It
holds 24 at once: after that, the oldest falls off for the newest. Nothing is
kept after you leave.

Meanwhile an elf strolls along the snow in front of the house, there and back.
As it passes the two round fun-house mirror windows, its reflection shows in
them: stretched tall and thin in the left one, squashed short and wide in the
right one, wobbling as fun-house mirrors do.

## On the map

The fun house stands on the map as a gingerbread house with a wonky roof. Its
drawing hangs on the front under a tall gold sign. Two elves queue at its door
and a third waves a ticket, a candy-cane lamp glows beside it, gumdrop string
lights shimmer along its eaves, a pennant flies from its crooked ridge and
smoke curls from its chimney.

## The code

- `attraction.ts`: the attraction, and the treats it remembers for one visit.
- `house.ts`: the house itself, its mirror windows and its gumdrop lights.
- `decorating.ts`: placing treats, and keeping them on the leaning house.
- `mirrors.ts`: the elf's stroll, and how each mirror stretches it.
- `stroller.ts`: drawing the elf, its reflections and the treats.
