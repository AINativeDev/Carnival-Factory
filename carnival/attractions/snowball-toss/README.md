# Snowball Toss

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A game you play, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a game booth
in the corner of a slab of snow, two back walls lit by a warm lamp, with green
and white awnings, and three gingerbread shelves with a tin snowman in a top
hat sliding to and fro along each. Two candy-cane prizes sway from the
awnings, and a counter stands at the front.

## How to play

Go inside, then tap or click where you want each snowball to go. It flies in
an arc from the counter and lands there. A snowball that lands on a snowman
knocks it down, and it stands back up after a moment. The higher the shelf,
the faster its snowman slides, so it scores more: 1 point for the bottom
shelf, 2 for the middle and 3 for the top.

A round is 10 snowballs. The scoreboard on the left shows this round's score,
your best round of this visit, and a dot for each snowball left. When the
round's last snowball lands, tap again to play another round.

With nobody playing, a snowball flies now and then on its own to knock a
snowman down, and it scores nothing.

## On the map

It stands as a fairground game stall with an elf at the front throwing
snowballs at the targets, two more queueing with their tickets, pennants on
its posts and candy-cane prizes under its striped header.
