# Snowball Toss

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A game you play, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a game booth
in the corner of a slab of snow, two back walls lit by a warm lamp, with green
and white awnings and a red pennant on its roof, and three gingerbread shelves
with a tin snowman in a top hat sliding to and fro along each. Two candy-cane
prizes sway from the awnings, a counter stands at the front, and snowflakes
drift across the booth with the wind.

## How to play

Go inside, then tap or click where you want each snowball to go. It flies in
an arc from the counter, and the wind bends it on the way, so it lands to one
side of where you tapped: aim upwind. A gold ring shows where you're aiming.

- **Targets.** A snowball that lands on a snowman knocks it down, and it
  stands back up after a moment. The higher the shelf, the faster its snowman
  slides and the more it scores: 1 point for the bottom shelf, 2 for the
  middle and 3 for the top.
- **Wind.** The sign at the top right says how hard it blows (calm, breeze or
  strong) with an arrow the way it blows, as long as it's strong. The pennant
  on the roof streams out with it. It gusts gently, and each round blows
  harder than the last, from a light breeze to a strong wind the other way.
- **Combos.** Two hits in a row score double, three or more triple, until a
  miss. The scoreboard shows the combo, and each hit pops its points where it
  landed; a miss bursts into snow.
- **Gold snowman.** Now and then one snowman turns gold for three seconds,
  glowing, with a "GOLD" tag over its hat. Hit it for 5 points, before the
  combo.
- **Rounds.** A game is three rounds of 8 snowballs, and the snowmen slide
  faster each round. When a round's last snowball lands, a card shows the
  round's score and the total; tap for the next round. After the third, the
  card shows the game's score and the best game of your visit ("New best!"
  when you beat it); tap to play again.

The scoreboard at the top left shows the game's score, the best of your
visit, the round, a dot for each snowball left and what to do next.

With nobody playing, a snowball flies now and then on its own, aimed against
the wind, to knock a snowman down, and it scores nothing. A game left alone
for half a minute goes back to that.

## On the map

It stands as a fairground game stall with an elf at the front throwing
snowballs at the targets, two more queueing with their tickets, pennants on
its posts and candy-cane prizes under its striped header.
