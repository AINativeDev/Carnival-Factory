# The Academy

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

It's the landmark for The Academy, the North Pole's own level
([`carnival/levels/the-academy/`](../../levels/the-academy/)). On the home map,
a click on it, or E or Enter beside it, takes the sleigh into the level rather
than into a room. Its own page, `#/attraction/the-academy`, still shows this
drawing in its room, with a **Start the Academy** button and a link to take
the sleigh inside.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
cream schoolhouse with a red pitched roof and two rows of lit windows, and a
bell tower on its ridge whose gold bell swings, under a red spire with a gold
star. On the map it stands as a pavilion with this drawing on its front.
