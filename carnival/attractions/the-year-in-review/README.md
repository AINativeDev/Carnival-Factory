# The Year in Review

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

It's the landmark for The Year in Review, the North Pole's own gallery room
([`carnival/levels/the-year-in-review/`](../../levels/the-year-in-review/)).
On the home map it stands as a gallery, and a click on it, or E or Enter
beside it, takes the sleigh into the room. Its own page,
`#/attraction/the-year-in-review`, still shows this drawing in its room, with
an **Add your answer** button and a link to take the sleigh inside.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
small cream gallery on a step of snow, four columns before its lit doors,
’26 → ’27 on its frieze under a pediment, a gold dome on the roof, and
confetti drifting slowly down to see the year out.
