# Frozen Pond

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): an oval
pond of ice sunk into a slab of snow, with snowy pines at the back, a lamp
throwing a warm pool across the ice and a shine drifting over it. Three
skaters in red, green and violet scarves trace a figure eight scratched into
the ice, a third of a lap apart, once every eight seconds, each with a soft
shadow.

## How to play

Step inside, and a fourth skater in a gold scarf is yours:

- **Steer** by moving the mouse, or a finger on a phone, over the pond. The
  skater glides after it and never leaves the ice.
- **Carve** as you go: the skater's blades leave a bright line in the ice that
  fades away over four seconds.
- **Spin** with a click or a tap: the skater turns round on the spot.
- **Skate a figure eight**, a loop one way and then a loop the other, and a
  ring of little gold stars sparkles over the skater.

When nobody is steering, the gold skater skates the figure eight with the others.
