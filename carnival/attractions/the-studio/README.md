# The Recording Studio

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

It's the landmark for The Recording Studio, the North Pole's own level
([`carnival/levels/the-studio/`](../../levels/the-studio/)). On the home map, a
click on it, or E or Enter beside it, takes the sleigh into the level rather
than into a room. Its own page, `#/attraction/the-studio`, still shows this
drawing in its room, with **Listen to the podcast** and **Watch on YouTube**
buttons and a link to take the sleigh inside.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
dark studio building with a lit door, a red **ON AIR** sign over it that pulses
bright and back, a warm window on its side, a satellite dish turning a little
on the roof, and an antenna sending waves out. On the map it stands as a
pavilion with this drawing on its front.
