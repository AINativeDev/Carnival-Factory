# Reindeer Carousel

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a
carousel on a round platform on a slab of snow, under a red and white
candy-cane canopy with a gold trim, a string of lantern lights and a gold ball
on top. Six reindeer with gold antlers and red noses go round it on gold poles,
once every nine seconds, rising and falling as they turn, and the canopy turns
with them. The ones at the back pass behind the gold centre column, fainter,
facing the other way, so you can see them come round. A soft glow chases round
the lantern lights as it turns.

## How to play

Step inside the carousel, where it fills the back wall, and use the mouse or
your finger:

- **Pick a reindeer to ride.** Tap a reindeer and an elf in green with a red
  hat sits on it and rides round. Tap another reindeer to move the elf there,
  or tap the elf's own reindeer to let it hop off.
- **Hold to speed it up.** Hold the mouse button or your finger down and the
  carousel gathers speed, up to three times as fast, with the lights chasing
  faster and the platform glowing a little warmer. The elf leans into the turn.
- **Let go to slow it down.** It eases gently back to its own pace.

The lights only ever swell softly, never flash, even at the fastest.
