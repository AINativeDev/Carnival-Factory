# Santa's Big Top

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): Santa's
workshop under a round big top in red and white stripes, standing on a slab of
snow, lit warm from the upper left. A gold scalloped hem and a string of
lantern lights run round the roof, and a green pennant flutters on the peak.
Elves in pointed green hats pop out of the tent's three glowing flaps in turn
and duck back in.

Preview it at `#/attraction/big-top`.
