# Santa's Big Top

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

Inside Santa's Big Top a circus show plays, in the
[diorama style](../../../docs/style.md): a ring with a red and white striped
curb on a slab of snow, under the tent's striped walls with lantern lights along
their tops, lit warm from the upper left.

- **The spotlight** follows your pointer, or your finger, across the ring, and
  sweeps round it on its own when nobody points.
- **Two juggling elves** at the back of the ring keep three balls each going
  round.
- **Tap** anywhere to send the acrobat elf off the springboard in a somersault
  over the ring, and to beat the drum at the ringside. A lamp lights over the
  drum for each beat of the drum roll.
- **Every fifth tap** is the big finish: a warm glow rises over the ring, gold
  stars drift up and the jugglers throw higher, then it all settles down again.

Step inside the Big Top to play: on the map a tap on it takes you in. The
lights fade in and out slowly, so nothing flashes.
