The Elf Engineer badge

For people who set up a Tessl factory on a repository of their own. Ask for it with the Elf Engineer form, and once it's granted, paste the snippet into your README. Bronze, silver and gold are for 1, 5 and 25 factory pull requests merged on your repository: ask again with the same form to move up.

The badges are plain image files, with no counters and nothing tracked.
