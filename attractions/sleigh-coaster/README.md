# Sleigh-Ride Coaster

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../docs/style.md): a
roller coaster with a candy-cane rail on wooden trestles, running across a
slab of snow from its left corner to its right, over a small bump, a big snowy
hill and a last bump. The hill's slopes are lit where they face the light.
Santa's red sleigh, with a sack and a present aboard and a warm lantern glow,
rides it from left to right every five seconds, tilting with the track,
slowing near the top of the hill and speeding up on the way down.

Preview it at `#/attraction/sleigh-coaster`.
