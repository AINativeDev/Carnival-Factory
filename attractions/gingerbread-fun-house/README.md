# Gingerbread Fun House

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../docs/style.md): a
gingerbread house on a slab of snow, lit on its left and shaded on its right,
with a chocolate roof edged in icing and a candy-cane door glowing warm. It
leans and sways a little, as a fun house should. Gumdrop lights along the
eaves glow and dim as a dark gap chases along the string, and its two round
mirror windows stretch and squash.

Preview it at `#/attraction/gingerbread-fun-house`.
