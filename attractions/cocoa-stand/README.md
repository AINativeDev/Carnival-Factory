# Hot Cocoa Stand

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A wooden stand with a red and yellow scalloped awning and a gold sign of three
cocoa drops. Four mugs with marshmallows sit on its snowy counter, each with
wisps of steam curling up and fading, and a lantern glows warm inside.

Preview it at `#/attraction/cocoa-stand`.
