# Snowball Toss

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A little diorama at dusk, in the [diorama style](../../docs/style.md): a game
booth in the corner of a slab of snow, two back walls lit by a warm lamp, with
green and white awnings, and a pyramid of six tin snowmen in top hats on a
gingerbread shelf. Every four seconds a snowball arcs in from the front and
knocks the top snowman over, and then it is stood back up for the next throw.
Two candy-cane prizes sway from the awnings, and a counter stands at the
front.
