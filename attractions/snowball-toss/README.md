# Snowball Toss

An attraction of the North Pole: it belongs to the map, not to a participant.

A game booth with a green and white awning and a pyramid of six tin snowmen in
top hats on a shelf. Every four seconds a snowball arcs in from the front and
knocks the top snowman over, and then it is stood back up for the next throw.
Two candy-cane prizes sway on either side.
