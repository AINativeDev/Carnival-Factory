# 0018. How the map says booths are community-made and not endorsed

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Issue: #117

## Context

Booths are made by participants and built by an AI factory with automatic
checks. They're best effort, can change or be removed, and don't speak for the
organisers. Nothing on the map says so today.

## Options under consideration

- **A. A line in the visit panel** under the owner (recommended).
- **B. A "Community booth" marker** on the hover tag and in the Booths panel.
- **C. An "About this carnival" page** and README section.
- **D. Share pages** that describe a booth as community-made.
- **E. An agreement on the request form**, with a code of conduct.

## Open questions

- The exact wording, and whether to name the company or "the organisers".
- A plus which of B to E?
- Our own code of conduct, or the Contributor Covenant?

## Decision

Not made yet. When it is, this record is updated to Accepted with the choice,
the options turned down and why, and its consequences.
